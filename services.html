Planning your signage

How to choose the right sign board for your business

The right signage depends on where it will be installed, how far away customers need to read it, the lighting conditions and your brand identity. Consider these factors before selecting a design.

Indoor signage

Indoor signs are used in reception areas, offices, retail spaces and showrooms. Acrylic lettering, reception logos and directional signs can help visitors identify departments and navigate a space.

Outdoor signage

Outdoor signs need to remain visible in changing daylight and weather conditions. Select suitable materials, lighting, mounting methods and finishes for the installation location.

Illuminated signage

LED and glow sign boards can improve visibility in the evening and in low-light locations. The appropriate lighting style depends on viewing distance, sign dimensions and the appearance you want.

Materials and finish

Consider materials, finish and installation conditions

Signage materials should be selected according to the design, installation surface and whether the sign will be used indoors or outdoors. Discuss the expected exposure, maintenance needs and lighting requirements before confirming the specification.

Acrylic

Acrylic is commonly used for clean lettering, logo panels and illuminated signage. Colour, thickness and finish can be selected to suit the design.

LED lighting

LED components can be used in illuminated letters and sign boards. Their placement and brightness should be planned around readability and the sign's intended viewing conditions.

Outdoor-grade specifications

For exterior installations, consider exposure to sunlight, rain, dust and wind, along with suitable electrical protection and secure mounting. The final specification should match the site conditions.

Before you order

Frequently asked questions

What affects signage cost?

Size, materials, lighting type, mounting height and design complexity can all affect cost.

Can you help choose a suitable sign?

Share a photo of the location, approximate dimensions and your branding requirements so suitable options can be discussed.

Are outdoor signs weather-resistant?

Outdoor signage should use materials and components suited to the exposure and installation location.

What information should I share to get a signage quotation?

Share your business name, signage type, approximate size, installation location and a photo of the site if available. You can also share your logo and preferred colours to help explain the design requirement.

Can signage be designed for indoor and outdoor use?

Yes. Signage can be planned for indoor spaces such as reception areas and showrooms, as well as outdoor locations such as shopfronts and building facades. Material and installation specifications should match the intended environment.

What is the difference between acrylic signage and channel letters?

Acrylic signage often uses shaped acrylic panels or letters, while channel letters are three-dimensional individual letters that can incorporate illumination. The suitable option depends on your brand design, mounting surface and visibility requirements.

Can I request custom sizes, colours and lettering?

Signage can be discussed around your required dimensions, brand colours, lettering style and installation space. Share your requirements so the appropriate options can be reviewed.
